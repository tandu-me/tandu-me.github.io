How Tandu works

Less scrolling, more meaning. The shape of it is simple.

1

Tell us about you

A short, honest profile — who you are, what matters to you, and what you're actually looking for.

2

Get a thoughtful match

Instead of a feed to swipe through, you receive introductions chosen with care — quality over quantity.

3

Chat, meet, see where it goes

Talk in the app at your own pace, then decide together when you're ready to meet.

Our approach

A few things we care about more than growth metrics.

Quality over quantity

We'd rather you get one good introduction than a hundred mediocre ones.

Privacy by default

Your information is used to help you meet people — not to build a profile of you for anyone else.

Built by a small team

Tandu is made by a small, independent team based in Israel. We read what you send us.

Be first to know when we launch

We're a small team getting the details right before opening things up. Leave your name and we'll reach out when there's something to try.
