Support

We're pre-launch, so this page will grow once the app is live.

What is Tandu?

Tandu is a matchmaking app that gives people a small number of thoughtfully chosen introductions instead of an endless feed to swipe through. We're currently finishing development ahead of launch.

When does Tandu launch?

We don't have a public date yet. Request early access on the home page and we'll let you know as soon as there's something to try.

I need help with my account or the app.

Email us at support@tandu.me and a real person will get back to you. Since we haven't launched publicly yet, response times may vary.

How is my data handled?

See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it's used.

I want to report a bug or safety concern.

Please email support@tandu.me with as much detail as you can — we read every message.
