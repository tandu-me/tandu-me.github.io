Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

This policy explains how Tandu Me LTD ("Tandu", "we", "us") handles personal information in connection with this website and, once available, the Tandu app. We are a pre-launch product; the app itself is not yet publicly available, so today this mostly concerns the information you choose to share with us directly (for example, by requesting early access).

Who we are

Tandu Me LTD is registered in Israel, with its registered address at Klil HaHoresh 274, Nes Harim, Israel 9988500. We can be reached at contact@tandu.me.

What we collect

  • Early access requests. If you email us to request early access, we keep the name, email address, and any other details you choose to include, so we can contact you when the app is ready.
  • Support correspondence. If you email support@tandu.me, we keep that correspondence to help you and to improve the product.
  • Basic site analytics. Standard, aggregated web server logs (such as page requests) may be collected by our hosting provider for security and reliability purposes.

We do not currently use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers on this website.

How we use it

We use the information above to respond to you, to let you know when Tandu is available, and to operate and improve this website and the upcoming app. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data retention

We keep early access and support correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and, if you consent, to notify you about launch. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see below).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how it's used. To exercise any of these rights, email contact@tandu.me and we'll respond as quickly as we can.

When the app launches

This policy will be expanded before public launch to cover the data the Tandu app itself collects (such as profile information and in-app messages) and how matching works at a high level. We will publish that update here before the app becomes generally available.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@tandu.me.
